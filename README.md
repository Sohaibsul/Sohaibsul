## Hi, I'm Sohaib Sulman 👋

I'm an MS Data Science student at **FAST-NUCES Islamabad**. I did my BS in Mathematics at **NUML**.

My research is about **trustworthy machine learning**: models that know when they might be wrong, keep working when the data changes, and hand uncertain cases to a person.

### 🔬 Research interests
- **Uncertainty quantification:** conformal prediction and calibration under distribution shift
- **Agentic AI:** LLM and VLM agents that use domain tools, with set-gated human-in-the-loop decisions
- **Medical imaging:** retinal disease classification and diabetic-retinopathy grading
- **Scientific ML:** gravitational-wave detector characterization (LIGO / Gravity Spy)

### 📌 Featured projects
| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [ConfTriage-GS](https://github.com/Sohaibsul/ConfTriage-GS) | Shows that conformal prediction for LIGO glitches fails silently across observing runs. Introduces class-conditional adaptive calibration (Mondrian-ACI) and retrieval-verified triage. |
